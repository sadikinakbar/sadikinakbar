# 📊 Manpower Planning & Workforce Efficiency

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates a practical approach to manpower planning and workforce efficiency within the hospitality industry, particularly for a mid-scale hotel operation.

The purpose of this case study is to analyze workforce requirements, optimize staffing structures, and improve operational efficiency while maintaining service quality.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze manpower requirements
- Improve workforce efficiency
- Optimize staffing allocation
- Support operational effectiveness
- Control labor costs
- Maintain service standards

---

## Case Study

### Hotel Profile

- Category: Hospitality Industry
- Job Cluster 
- Occupancy Range: 40% – 60%
- Operation: 24 Hours

---

## HR Analysis

The manpower analysis focuses on:

- Front Office
- Housekeeping
- Food & Beverage
- Kitchen
- Engineering
- Security
- Accounting
- Human Resources
- Sales & Marketing

---

## Workforce Strategy

Different employment models may be applied based on operational needs:

- Permanent Employees
- PKWT Employees
- Daily Workers
- Casual Workers

This strategy supports flexibility while maintaining productivity.

---

## Key Findings

- Workforce efficiency can improve operational performance.
- Flexible staffing models can support cost optimization.
- Proper manpower planning reduces overstaffing and understaffing risks.
- HR analytics support better decision-making.
