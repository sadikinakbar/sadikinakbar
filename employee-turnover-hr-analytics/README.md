# 📈 Employee Turnover & HR Analytics

## Overview

A practical HR case study focused on analyzing employee turnover, identifying resignation patterns, and developing practical employee retention strategies.

This project demonstrates how HR data can be used to identify potential organizational issues and support management decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Measure employee turnover
- Identify major resignation factors
- Analyze employee retention risks
- Identify potential leadership-related issues
- Develop practical retention strategies
- Support management decision-making

---

## 📊 Turnover Analysis Framework

Employee turnover should be analyzed not only by the number of employees leaving, but also by the underlying reasons.

Key indicators include:

- Monthly turnover rate
- Number of resignations
- Voluntary vs. involuntary turnover
- Length of service
- Department
- Position
- Resignation reason
- Leadership-related factors
- Compensation considerations
- Employee relations issues

---

## 🔍 Example Case Analysis

A sample turnover review identified several potential resignation factors:

| Factor | HR Interpretation |
|---|---|
| Compensation | Employee seeks better income opportunity |
| Leadership | Relationship with supervisor or HOD |
| Management Style | Leadership approach affects employee experience |
| Performance / Human Error | Employee capability or performance issue |
| Career Opportunity | Employee seeks better career development |

*All examples are generalized and do not represent confidential company data.*

---

## 🧠 Root Cause Analysis

Turnover should be evaluated using a combination of quantitative and qualitative information.

### Quantitative Analysis

- Turnover percentage
- Departmental turnover
- Tenure distribution
- Monthly resignation trend
- Position-level turnover

### Qualitative Analysis

- Exit interview
- Employee feedback
- Supervisor evaluation
- Employee complaints
- Workplace relationship
- Compensation concerns
- Career development opportunities

---

## 💡 HR Recommendations

### 1. Leadership Evaluation

Conduct regular evaluation and coaching for supervisors and HODs.

### 2. Exit Interview

Standardize exit interviews to identify recurring organizational issues.

### 3. Employee Feedback

Create a structured channel for employees to communicate concerns.

### 4. Retention Strategy

Identify critical positions and employees with high replacement costs.

### 5. Career Development

Provide clear development and training opportunities.

### 6. Compensation Review

Benchmark compensation when turnover is consistently associated with income expectations.
