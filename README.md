## Overview

This project demonstrates how HR can align workforce requirements with operational workload, business demand, and service standards.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

## 🔄 Workforce Strategy

A combination of employment models can be considered according to operational requirements:

- Permanent / Core Employees
- PKWT Employees
- Daily Workers
- Flexible / Casual Workforce

The objective is not simply to reduce headcount, but to achieve the **right workforce structure for the actual business demand**.

---

## 💡 HR Recommendations

1. Maintain core employees for critical operational functions.
2. Use flexible workforce models for demand-driven positions.
3. Adjust manpower requirements according to occupancy and workload.
4. Review productivity and workload periodically.
5. Avoid excessive staffing during low-demand periods.
6. Ensure workforce efficiency does not compromise service quality or employee compliance.
